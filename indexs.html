Frequently Asked Questions

How does myOne generate daily profits?
myOne aggregates member investments and distributes capital to vetted businesses worldwide across industries like technology, retail, energy, and finance. These businesses operate profitably and generate returns using your distributed capital. A portion of these business profits is calculated and distributed back to our members daily.
Is my initial deposit locked?
No lock-up periods. Your principal investment can be withdrawn at any time, 24/7. You maintain complete control of your capital while earning daily returns from global business distributions. Withdraw whenever you choose.
Do I have to recruit people to earn?
No. You earn daily returns on your investment automatically from the global business distribution network. Growing a community is optional and creates additional income, but your base returns work passively without recruiting anyone.
What cryptocurrencies are supported?
We support BTC, ETH, USDT (TRC20/ERC20/BEP20), USDC, BNB, and XRP. More major cryptocurrencies are being added continuously as we expand our distribution network.
